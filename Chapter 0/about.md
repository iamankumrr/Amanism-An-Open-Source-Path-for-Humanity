## 0.1 What is Amanism?

**Amanism** is an open-source spiritual philosophy based on the belief in **one universal God — Energy**.

The word **Amanism** comes from **"Aman"**, meaning **peace**.

Amanism seeks to improve the way people see themselves, other people, nature, and the world.

It is inspired by the idea that humanity has accumulated valuable wisdom through thousands of years of religions, philosophies, cultures, scientific discoveries, and personal experiences.

Amanism attempts to bring together the **good, meaningful, and beneficial ideas found throughout humanity** while respecting the traditions from which those ideas originated.

> **Amanism is not created to replace other religions. It is created to learn from humanity.**

---

# 0.2 The Meaning of "Aman" 🕊️

**Aman = Peace**

Peace is the foundation of Amanism.

But peace does not only mean the absence of war or conflict.

For Amanism, peace can include:

- Peace within ourselves
- Peace between individuals
- Peace between communities
- Peace between different religions
- Peace with nature
- Peace through understanding
- Peace through acceptance of differences

Amanism aims to encourage people to create peace both **inside themselves and around themselves**.

> **Peace within. Peace among humanity. Peace with nature.**

---

# 0.3 One God — Energy ⚡

Amanism believes in **one universal God, called Energy**.

Energy represents the universal reality through which existence is connected.

Amanism does not require every person to understand Energy in exactly the same way.

Different people may have different philosophical understandings of God, existence, consciousness, and the universe.

The idea of Energy is therefore open to philosophical exploration.

> **One universe. One existence. One universal Energy.**

Amanism should not claim that modern physics has scientifically proven "Energy" to be a conscious God. The concept is a **spiritual and philosophical belief**, while scientific questions about the physical universe remain subjects for scientific investigation.

---

# 0.4 Direct Connection With God 🧘

Amanism believes that every individual can seek a direct connection with Energy.

No human being is required to stand between an individual and God.

Amanism does not require:

- A priest
- A pandit
- A maulvi
- A pastor
- A guru
- Any religious intermediary

Teachers, philosophers, elders, and knowledgeable people may share their understanding, but they do not possess exclusive access to Energy.

> **No one stands between you and Energy.**

A person's spiritual journey is their own.

---

# 0.5 Freedom of Choice 🕊️

**Nothing in Amanism should be forced upon a person.**

Amanism offers ideas, wisdom, practices, and perspectives.

Every person is free to decide:

- What they believe
- What they do not believe
- Which teachings they accept
- Which teachings they question
- Which teachings they reject
- Which practices they follow
- Which practices they do not follow
- Whether they want to participate in Amanism

A person should never be threatened, punished, discriminated against, or pressured for rejecting an Amanist idea.

> **You are free to accept. You are free to question. You are free to reject.**

Amanism can suggest a path.

**Only the individual can choose whether to walk it.**

---

# 0.6 Respect for Every Religion 🤝

Amanism respects **every religion and belief system**.

It does not exist to attack, insult, or discriminate against any religious tradition.

Humanity has developed countless religious and philosophical traditions throughout history, and many contain valuable ideas about:

- Compassion
- Discipline
- Peace
- Justice
- Self-control
- Service
- Forgiveness
- Knowledge
- Responsibility
- Spirituality
- Human relationships

Amanism seeks to learn from these contributions.

Examples include:

- Hinduism
- Buddhism
- Jainism
- Sikhism
- Islam
- Christianity
- Judaism
- Taoism
- Indigenous traditions
- Other spiritual traditions
- Secular philosophies
- Human experience
- Scientific thought

Respecting a religion does **not** mean accepting every teaching from that religion.

Amanism encourages people to study teachings in their proper historical and cultural context and decide for themselves what they find meaningful.

> **Learn from everyone. Respect everyone. Question everything.**

---

# 0.7 The Purpose of Amanism 🌍

The central purpose of Amanism is to **improve human beings and improve the environment in which humanity lives**.

Amanism seeks to encourage people to develop a broader vision of the world.

A person should try to see beyond:

- Religious divisions
- Caste divisions
- National divisions
- Economic divisions
- Cultural differences
- Social status
- Personal prejudice

The goal is not to eliminate differences.

The goal is to learn how to **live peacefully despite differences**.

### Amanism seeks a movement:

**From hatred → to understanding**

**From discrimination → to equality**

**From ignorance → to learning**

**From selfishness → to compassion**

**From division → to cooperation**

**From blind acceptance → to thoughtful understanding**

**From unnecessary consumption → to responsible living**

**From helplessness → to constructive action**

---

# 0.8 The Amanist Way of Life 🌱

Amanism proposes a simple cycle:

> **Learn → Reflect → Improve → Help → Repeat**

### Learn

Learn from:

- People
- Books
- Religions
- Philosophies
- Cultures
- Science
- Nature
- History
- Personal experience
- Mistakes

### Reflect

Think about what you learned and how you behaved.

### Improve

Identify mistakes and try to become better.

### Help

Use your:

- Time
- Knowledge
- Skills
- Resources
- Opportunities

to help others and improve your surroundings.

### Repeat

Continue learning and improving throughout life.

This is not a compulsory rule.

It is a **suggested way of living**.

---

# 0.9 Daily Revision 🧘

One of the central practices proposed by Amanism is **Daily Revision**.

Daily Revision is a period of meditation and reflection in which a person reviews their day.

A person may ask:

> What did I learn today?

> What good did I do?

> Did I hurt anyone?

> Did I unnecessarily hurt myself?

> Did I harm nature?

> What made me genuinely happy?

> What made me unhappy?

> What mistake did I make?

> What can I learn from that mistake?

> What can I improve tomorrow?

The purpose is not to create guilt.

The purpose is **awareness and improvement**.

> **Every day is a chapter. Every night is a revision. Every tomorrow is another opportunity to improve.**

---

# 0.10 Happiness ❤️

Amanism encourages people to understand their happiness rather than simply chase it.

During Daily Revision, a person may reflect on moments that brought genuine happiness.

They can ask:

> **Why did this moment make me happy?**

Perhaps happiness came from:

- Helping someone
- Learning something
- Spending time with family
- Friendship
- Nature
- Achievement
- Creativity
- Rest
- Personal growth

Understanding what creates meaningful happiness can help a person build a more fulfilling life.

# 0.11 Humanity Above Division 🌍

Amanism emphasizes the idea that human beings share a common humanity despite their differences.

No person should be considered inferior because of:

- Religion
- Caste
- Race
- Nationality
- Language
- Gender
- Culture
- Economic status
- Family background
- Personal beliefs

Differences can exist without becoming reasons for hatred.

> **Different people. Different beliefs. One humanity.**

---

# 0.12 Compassion and Helping Others 🤲

Amanism encourages compassion toward people and living beings.

Helping others can take many forms.

It does not always require money.

A person can help through:

- Knowledge
- Education
- Time
- Skills
- Food
- Shelter
- Emotional support
- Volunteering
- Protecting nature
- Creating opportunities

The ability to help will differ from person to person.

Amanism does not require someone to sacrifice their basic needs in order to help others.

Instead, it encourages people to help **according to their ability**.

---

# 0.13 Wealth and Responsibility 💰

Amanism does not consider earning money wrong.

People can work, build businesses, develop skills, create wealth, and improve their lives.

Money can provide:

- Food
- Housing
- Education
- Healthcare
- Security
- Opportunities
- Independence

However, Amanism encourages people to question whether endless accumulation of wealth should become the ultimate purpose of life.

A person may aim to fulfill their:

- Basic needs
- Family responsibilities
- Future responsibilities
- Reasonable personal goals

Once a person's essential needs and responsibilities are reasonably fulfilled, they may choose to use their additional:

**Time + Knowledge + Skills + Resources**

to help people in need or contribute to society.

This is an encouragement, not a compulsory command.

> **Earn responsibly. Live meaningfully. Give when you can.**

---

# 0.14 Food and Nutrition 🍎

Amanism does not discriminate against people because of what they eat.

People may choose vegetarian or non-vegetarian food according to their:

- Nutritional needs
- Health
- Culture
- Personal beliefs
- Personal preferences
- Circumstances

Amanism does not require everyone to follow the same diet.

The general philosophy encourages:

**Nutrition + Moderation + Responsibility + Respect for life and nature**

A person should try to understand their nutritional requirements and make informed choices.

Humanity is part of nature.

Amanism encourages people to consider how their actions affect:

- Air
- Water
- Soil
- Animals
- Plants
- Ecosystems
- Future generations

Possible actions include:

- Reducing unnecessary waste
- Keeping surroundings clean
- Using resources responsibly
- Protecting animals
- Protecting biodiversity
- Planting and caring for trees
- Reducing unnecessary environmental damage

> **Leave the world better than you found it.**

Again, these are principles to consider—not commands that remove personal freedom.

# 0.16 Science and Reason 🔬

Amanism respects:

- Science
- Evidence
- Reason
- Critical thinking
- Curiosity
- Questioning
- Open discussion

Amanism encourages people to explore two dimensions:

### The external world

Through:

**Science + Observation + Evidence + Experimentation**

### The internal world

Through:

**Meditation + Reflection + Experience + Self-observation**

Amanism does not require spiritual beliefs to override reliable scientific evidence.

When Amanism makes claims about the physical world, those claims should remain open to investigation and correction.

# 0.17 Freedom of Thought 🧠

Amanism encourages independent thinking.

A person should be able to:

- Ask questions
- Disagree
- Debate respectfully
- Change their mind
- Explore other religions
- Study philosophy
- Study science
- Accept an Amanist teaching
- Reject an Amanist teaching
- Leave Amanism

No person should be punished for thinking differently.

> **A belief should not be afraid of questions.**

---

# 0.18 No Religious Hierarchy

Amanism does not require a religious hierarchy.

There is no requirement for a special class of people who possess greater access to Energy.

Someone may be:

- A teacher
- A researcher
- A philosopher
- A meditation guide
- A community volunteer

But these roles do not give a person greater spiritual value than anyone else.

> **Knowledge can be shared. Spiritual access cannot be owned.**

---

# 0.19 Amanism Is Not a Rejection of Religion

Amanism should not be understood as an attack on existing religions.

Its purpose is to explore humanity's accumulated wisdom.

Amanism can learn from religious teachings while allowing individuals to maintain their existing religious identities if they wish.

A person can study Amanism while remaining:

- Hindu
- Muslim
- Christian
- Sikh
- Buddhist
- Jain
- Jewish
- Taoist
- Another faith
- Non-religious

Amanism does not require someone to abandon their previous identity.

> **Understanding another tradition does not require abandoning your own.**

---

# 0.20 What Amanism Does Not Claim

Amanism does not claim:

- That every religious teaching is correct
- That every Amanist idea is correct
- That the founder is always correct
- That Amanism has discovered every truth
- That everyone must become an Amanist
- That spiritual practices must be forced
- That science should be rejected
- That one culture is superior to another
- That one person has exclusive access to Energy

Amanism remains open to questioning and improvement.

# 0.22 The Founder

The founder of Amanism is the **initiator of the project**, not an unquestionable authority.

The founder can propose ideas.

The community can question them.

Contributors can improve them.

Future generations can revise them.

> **Amanism should be bigger than its founder.**

No individual should own the truth.

# 0.23 How Amanism Can Evolve 🔄

Amanism can use an open development process.

A proposed teaching can go through:

**Idea → Discussion → Research → Criticism → Improvement → Community Review → Adoption**

And even after adoption, it can be reconsidered if better reasoning or evidence becomes available.

This means Amanism can evolve with humanity.

> **What we believe today should remain open to what we learn tomorrow.**
